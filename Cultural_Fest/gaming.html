About Gaming & Coding

Play Sharp. Build Bold.

The Gaming & Coding Arena celebrates the intersection of technology and creativity. From esports showdowns that test reflexes and teamwork to frontend design sprints and coding competitions — this arena is for the digitally creative.

"Skill, strategy, and imagination meet here."
🎯

Competitive Play

Valorant, EA FC, mobile gaming — esports showdowns that test every reflex.

💻

Creative Code

Frontend sprints, coding challenges — build and solve under pressure.

🧩

Team Strategy

Squad coordination, tactical thinking — victory through collaboration.

Gaming & Coding Events

Competitions

🎯

Valorant Squad Clash

  • Format5v5 team esports tournament
  • Team Size5 players + 1 substitute
  • DurationBest of 3 maps per match
  • VenueComputer Lab / Gaming Zone

Rules & Guidelines

  • Teams register together as a squad.
  • Standard Valorant competitive rules apply throughout.
  • Participants must use lab systems provided or bring own laptops (subject to organiser approval).
  • Tournament bracket format; advancement judged on match wins.
Register for Valorant Squad Clash →
⚽

EA FC Showdown

  • Format1v1 knockout tournament
  • Team Size1 participant
  • DurationStandard 6-minute halves per match
  • VenueGaming Zone

Rules & Guidelines

  • Current season of EA FC used for all matches.
  • Participants use controllers provided by the organisers.
  • Home/away assigned by random draw.
  • Standard match rules apply; penalty shootout decides drawn matches.
  • Tournament bracket format throughout.
Register for EA FC Showdown →
📱

Mobile Gaming Arena

  • Format4-player squad tournament (BGMI) or 1v1 (Clash Royale)
  • Team Size1–4 participants (event-specific)
  • DurationVaries by round
  • VenueDesignated Outdoor Gaming Area

Rules & Guidelines

  • Participants use their own smartphones.
  • Specific game titles announced by organisers prior to the event.
  • Devices must be fully charged and accounts active before the event begins.
  • No external accessories that provide an unfair competitive advantage.
  • Judged on match results in each respective game title.
Register for Mobile Gaming Arena →
💻

Frontend Design Sprint

  • FormatIndividual timed web design
  • Team Size1 participant
  • Duration3 hours (on-site)
  • VenueComputer Lab

Rules & Guidelines

  • Participants receive a design brief at the start of the sprint.
  • Create a static HTML/CSS webpage from scratch within the time limit.
  • No frameworks, UI libraries, templates, or AI-assisted tools permitted.
  • Judged on visual design quality, responsiveness approach, semantic HTML, creativity, and completeness.
Register for Frontend Design Sprint →
🧑‍💻

Coding Challenge

  • FormatIndividual or pair competitive programming
  • Team Size1–2 participants
  • Duration2–3 hours
  • VenueComputer Lab

Rules & Guidelines

  • Problems range from easy to hard — algorithmic, data structures, and logic-based.
  • Any standard programming language accepted: C++, Java, or Python.
  • An online judge platform will be used for automated evaluation.
  • Internet access restricted to official language documentation only.
  • Ranked by number of problems solved; time taken used as tiebreaker.
