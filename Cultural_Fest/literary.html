Words That Stay With You.

Literary Events

Literary Competitions

💬

Debate Championship

Parliamentary debate (team format)
2 participants per team
45–60 minutes per round
Seminar Hall A

Rules & Guidelines

  • British parliamentary or Asian parliamentary format followed throughout.
  • Topics announced 15 minutes before each round begins.
  • Teams alternate between proposition and opposition roles.
  • Judged on argumentation quality, rebuttals, and logical structure.
  • Clarity and delivery of speech are key evaluation criteria.

🎤

Slam Poetry

Individual spoken word performance
1 participant
3–5 minutes
Campus Courtyard / Open Mic Stage

Rules & Guidelines

  • Original compositions only — no published or borrowed works.
  • Any language accepted: Hindi, English, or regional languages.
  • No props, instruments, or costumes (minimal use permitted).
  • Poetry must be performed entirely from memory.
  • Judged on content, delivery, originality, emotional impact, and rhythm.

Creative Writing

Timed individual writing
1 participant
90 minutes (on-site)
Seminar Hall B

Rules & Guidelines

  • Topics provided on the day of the event.
  • Choose one genre at the start: short story, flash fiction, or poetry.
  • Participants must bring their own pens and paper.
  • No electronic devices permitted during the competition.
  • Judged on originality, language, narrative structure, and depth of thought.
Register for Creative Writing →
🧠

Quiz League

Team quiz competition
2–3 participants
60–90 minutes per round
Auditorium

Rules & Guidelines

  • Multiple rounds covering general knowledge, Indian culture, history, science, literature, and current affairs.
  • Both written and buzzer-based rounds included.
  • No electronic aids or devices permitted.
  • Tie-breaking rounds held as needed.
  • Judged on accuracy and speed of response.

🎙️

Open Mic

Individual or duo open performance
1–2 participants
3–5 minutes
Campus Courtyard Stage

Rules & Guidelines

  • Any form of spoken word content — poetry, storytelling, personal essay, comedy, or commentary.
  • Any language is welcome and encouraged.
  • Content must be respectful and non-offensive to all audiences.
  • No competitive judging — purely a celebration of voice and expression.
  • Sign-ups available on the day of the event at the venue.
