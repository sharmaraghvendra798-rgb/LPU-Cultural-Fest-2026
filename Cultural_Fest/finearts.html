About Fine Arts

Imagine. Create. Leave a Mark.

The Fine Arts Arena at LPU Cultural Fest 2026 is a celebration of visual creativity in all its richness. From the precise lines of live sketching to the bold colours of rangoli, from the storytelling of photography to the expressive transformation of face painting — this arena invites every visual artist to create freely.

"Every blank surface holds a new possibility."

Fine Arts Events

Fine Arts Competitions

✏️

Live Sketching

  • 🎯 Format: Individual timed sketching
  • 👤 Team Size: 1 participant
  • ⏱️ Duration: 60–90 minutes (on-site)
  • 📍 Venue: Arts Pavilion

Rules & Guidelines

  • Participants bring their own materials (pencil, charcoal, pen, etc.)
  • Topic / subject announced at the start of the event
  • A3 size paper provided at the venue
  • No digital tools permitted
  • Judged on technique, composition, likeness / interpretation, and detail

🖼️

Poster Design

  • 🎯 Format: Individual timed design
  • 👤 Team Size: 1 participant
  • ⏱️ Duration: 90 minutes (on-site)
  • 📍 Venue: Arts Pavilion

Rules & Guidelines

  • Participants may use paints, markers, colour pencils, or mixed media
  • Topic announced at the start of the event
  • A2 or A3 size sheet provided at the venue
  • Poster must include a legible title or message
  • Judged on visual impact, creativity, colour use, and message clarity
Register for Poster Design →
🌸

Rangoli

  • 🎯 Format: Individual or team
  • 👥 Team Size: 1–3 participants
  • ⏱️ Duration: 60–120 minutes (on-site)
  • 📍 Venue: Cultural Grounds (floor space)

Rules & Guidelines

  • Traditional rangoli materials (coloured powder, flower petals, rice flour) provided
  • Design area allocated per team at the venue
  • Theme based on Indian culture, nature, or festival
  • Participants may also bring their own materials
  • Judged on intricacy, symmetry, colour harmony, and cultural relevance
Register for Rangoli →
🎨

Face Painting

  • 🎯 Format: Pair competition (one artist, one model)
  • 👥 Team Size: 2 participants
  • ⏱️ Duration: 45–60 minutes
  • 📍 Venue: Arts Pavilion

Rules & Guidelines

  • One participant paints the other's face
  • Theme announced at the start of the event
  • Safe, skin-tested face paints only — participants are responsible for their own materials
  • Judged on creativity, technical skill, completeness, and theme adherence
Register for Face Painting →
✂️

Collage Art

  • 🎯 Format: Individual
  • 👤 Team Size: 1 participant
  • ⏱️ Duration: 90 minutes (on-site)
  • 📍 Venue: Arts Pavilion

Rules & Guidelines

  • Pre-collected newspaper/magazine clippings, fabric scraps, or found materials allowed (must be brought by participant)
  • Adhesive and base board provided at the venue
  • Theme given at the start of the event
  • No digital printing permitted
  • Judged on concept, visual balance, use of materials, and storytelling
Register for Collage Art →
📷

Photography Walk

  • 🎯 Format: Individual
  • 👤 Team Size: 1 participant
  • ⏱️ Duration: 2-hour campus walk, then submission
  • 📍 Venue: Campus (designated zones)

Rules & Guidelines

  • Any camera or smartphone permitted
  • Participants capture images within designated campus zones during allotted time
  • Submit 3 best images for judging
  • Theme announced at the start of the event
  • No heavy post-processing (basic exposure/crop adjustments only)
  • Judged on composition, lighting, storytelling, and thematic relevance
