✦ Helpful Checklist

Before You Register

Follow these essential pointers to ensure a smooth, hassle-free festival entry:

  • ✓
    Choose your preferred event category

    Explore our creative arenas — Dance, Music, Dramatics, Literary, Fine Arts, and Gaming — to select your stage.

  • ✓
    Keep your LPU registration number ready

    Have your valid 8–12 digit university registration number on hand for verification.

  • ✓
    Team captains should enter accurate participant details

    For duo, ensemble, and squad competitions, ensure all member names and sizes are entered correctly.

  • ✓
    Check the schedule page for event timings

    Review the timeline to verify that your selected events do not conflict across the three days. View Festival Schedule →
