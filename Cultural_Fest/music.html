About Music

Every Note Finds Its Place.

The Music Arena at LPU Cultural Fest 2026 is a space where every sonic tradition is honoured. From the intimacy of an acoustic set to the explosive energy of a band battle, from classical precision to the rhythmic creativity of beatbox and rap — every form of music is welcome here.

"Find your frequency and let it be heard."
🎤

Vocal Expression

Solo vocals, ghazals, rap — every voice carries a story worth hearing.

🎻

Instrumental Energy

Acoustic, classical, folk — instruments that speak louder than words.

🎸

Band Culture

Full bands, jam sessions, battle of bands — where sound meets collective fire.

Music Competitions

🎤

Solo Vocals

Format
Individual vocal performance
Team Size
1 participant
Duration
3–5 minutes
Venue
Main Auditorium Stage

Rules

Any language or genre (Hindustani, Carnatic, Western, folk, pop, ghazal); backing track allowed; live instruments may optionally accompany; judged on pitch accuracy, diction, emotional delivery, and stage presence.

Acoustic Unplugged

Format
Solo or duo performance
Team Size
1–2 participants
Duration
4–6 minutes
Venue
Campus Courtyard Stage

Rules

Acoustic instruments only (guitar, piano, violin, etc.); no amplification beyond basic sound reinforcement; original compositions or covers both accepted; judged on musicality, tone, and performance intimacy.

Instrumental Jam

Format

Team Size
1–4 participants
Duration
4–7 minutes
Venue
Music Hall

Rules

Battle of Bands

Format
Group band performance
Team Size
3–8 members
Duration
8–12 minutes
Venue
Open Air Amphitheatre

Rules

Full band format (minimum lead, rhythm, bass, drums); any genre; original songs or covers allowed; sound check slot allocated before performance; judged on musical tightness, energy, and audience engagement.

Rap & Beatbox

Format
Solo or duo
Team Size
1–2 participants
Duration
3–5 minutes
Venue
Campus Plaza

Rules

Original rap compositions strongly encouraged; beatbox may accompany or be standalone; any language (Hindi, English, regional); no offensive content; judged on flow, lyrical creativity, rhythm, and vocal control.

Folk Melody Showcase

Format
Solo or group (up to 5)
Team Size
1–5 participants
Duration
4–7 minutes
Venue
Cultural Grounds

Rules

Must represent a recognised Indian folk music tradition (e.g., Baul, Lavani, Bihu, Rajasthani folk, Maand); traditional instruments preferred; costumes encouraged; judged on authenticity, expression, and cultural presentation.
