About Dance

Move. Feel. Belong.

Dance at LPU Cultural Fest 2026 is a celebration of movement in all its forms. From the disciplined grace of classical Indian dance to the raw energy of street styles, from the storytelling of folk traditions to the collaborative power of group choreography — every form of movement finds its stage here.

"Every beat is an invitation to express."
🩰

Classical Grace

Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Odissi — disciplined forms that blend rhythm with devotion.

🪔

Folk Roots

Bhangra, Garba, Bihu — stories of land and people told through movement.

🕺

Street Energy

Breaking, popping, freestyle — raw expression powered by beat and body.

Sample Rules

Group Choreography

Group performance
5–15 participants
6–10 minutes
Open Air Amphitheatre

Sample Rules

  • Team must register together with a team name; any style or fusion theme accepted.
  • Formations, synchrony, and storytelling through movement are judged.
  • Live drumming accompaniment optionally permitted.

Folk Fusion

Solo or group (up to 8)
1–8 participants
4–7 minutes
Cultural Grounds

Sample Rules

  • Must incorporate at least one recognisable Indian folk dance tradition (e.g., Bhangra, Garba, Bihu, Lavani).
  • Fusion with contemporary elements encouraged; traditional attire preferred.
  • Judged on authenticity and creative interpretation.
Go to Registration →
🕺

Street Dance Face-Off

Battle (head-to-head rounds)
1–3 participants
60–90 seconds per round
Campus Plaza

Sample Rules

  • Freestyle format; styles include breaking, popping, locking, krump, waacking.
  • Rounds judged by panel on musicality, creativity, and execution; no pre-planned choreography.
  • Music selected by organisers.

Classical Expressions

Solo performance
1 participant
5–8 minutes
Main Auditorium Stage

Sample Rules

  • Recognised classical Indian dance forms only (Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Odissi, Kuchipudi, Manipuri, Mohiniyattam, Sattriya, Kathakali).
  • Live or recorded accompaniment; traditional costume mandatory.
  • Judged on technique, abhinaya, and rhythm.
