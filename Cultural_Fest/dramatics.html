About Dramatics

Where Stories Come Alive.

Dramatics at LPU Cultural Fest 2026 is where literature becomes motion, emotion becomes dialogue, and spaces transform into stages. From the urgency of street theatre to the stillness of mime — every dramatic form is an act of courage and connection.

"The stage belongs to every storyteller."
🎭

Stagecraft

Full productions, street plays, set design — theatre built from vision and teamwork.

🗣️

Powerful Voices

Monologues, dialogue, improv — spoken word that commands attention.

🤫

Living Characters

Mime, movement, expression — storytelling beyond spoken language.

Dramatics Competitions

🗣️

Nukkad Natak

Group Street Theatre
Team Size
5–12 participants
Duration
10–15 minutes
Venue
Campus Grounds (open space)

Rulebook

  • No stage — performance in an open public space on campus.
  • Props must be carried by the team; no external setups permitted.
  • Topic must be socially relevant: social issues, environment, heritage, etc.
  • No amplification or microphone use allowed.
  • Judged on message clarity, energy, crowd engagement, and ensemble coordination.

Stage Play

Full Theatrical Production
Team Size
4–15 members
Duration
15–20 minutes
Venue
Main Auditorium

Rulebook

  • Original script or adaptation of a published work (with proper credit).
  • Sets, lighting, and costumes are encouraged within venue limits.
  • Script must be submitted for approval before the event date.
  • Judged on direction, acting, production design, and narrative cohesion.

🎤

Mono Acting

Solo Performance
Team Size
1 participant
Duration
3–5 minutes
Venue
Main Auditorium Stage

Rulebook

  • Single performer portraying one or more characters; no dialogue partners.
  • Props and costume are permitted.
  • Performance may be in any language.
  • Original or adapted monologue — adaptation must be credited.
  • Judged on character depth, vocal range, body language, and emotional authenticity.
Register for Mono Acting →
🤫

Mime Performance

Solo or Duo
Team Size
1–2 participants
Duration
3–6 minutes
Venue
Main Auditorium Stage

Rulebook

  • No spoken words — expression entirely through gesture, movement, and facial expression.
  • Background music is permitted but not required.
  • Costume is encouraged to support the narrative.
  • Judged on clarity of narrative, physicality, and emotional communication.

Improv Arena

Group Improv Performance
Team Size
3–6 participants
Duration
8–12 minutes (scenario-based)
Venue
Black Box Theatre / Studio Space

Rulebook

  • Scenarios are provided by judges on the spot — no preparation allowed.
  • Team must respond spontaneously to all given prompts.
  • Judged on creativity, team listening, comic or dramatic timing, and audience connection.
